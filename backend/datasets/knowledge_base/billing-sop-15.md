# Billing Dispute Review SOP

## Purpose
This PakSim demo SOP defines review of disputed charges, balance deductions and billing complaints.

## Investigation
Verify the transaction or package reference, charge date, amount and account context.

## Resolution
Explain the verified billing outcome and apply only approved adjustment or refund rules. Never promise compensation before eligibility is verified.

## Escalation
Repeated unexplained deductions, high-value disputes or suspected unauthorized transactions must follow the relevant escalation rule.

## Audit
Record the evidence reviewed, decision and customer-facing resolution.
