# Packages & Bundles Support Policy

## Scope
This approved PakSim policy covers packages & bundles complaints handled by Customer Support.

## Standard handling
Verify the customer identity and collect the relevant service or transaction details. Apply the approved rule matrix and do not make unsupported promises.

## Categories
Package Activation, Package Cancellation, Package Renewal, Wrong Package

## Service level
Default target: 48 hours.

## Safety
Escalate security-sensitive or otherwise mandatory cases according to the rule matrix.
