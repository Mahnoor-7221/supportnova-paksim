# SIM Blocking and Lost/Stolen Procedure (PakSim)

## Scope
Applies when a customer reports a lost, stolen, or snatched phone/SIM, or requests an immediate security block.

## Important
- Only authorized support staff can complete a permanent SIM block in the backend after identity verification.
- The AI assistant must never claim that a SIM has already been blocked unless the backend confirms the action.

## Customer guidance (immediate)
1. Confirm the mobile number to be protected.
2. Advise the customer to file an FIR at the nearest police station and keep a copy for duplicate SIM issuance.
3. Advise visiting a PakSim franchise with original CNIC for biometric verification and duplicate SIM.
4. If mobile wallets or banks are linked to the number, advise the customer to notify those providers separately.

## Priority
Lost/stolen and misuse reports are treated as **Urgent / P1** and must appear in the agent urgent queue.
