# Balance & Billing Support Policy

## Scope
This approved PakSim policy covers balance & billing complaints handled by Billing & Payments.

## Standard handling
Verify the customer identity and collect the relevant service or transaction details. Apply the approved rule matrix and do not make unsupported promises.

## Categories
Wrong Deduction, Unexpected Charge, Recharge Problem, Billing Dispute

## Service level
Default target: 24 hours.

## Safety
Escalate security-sensitive or otherwise mandatory cases according to the rule matrix.
