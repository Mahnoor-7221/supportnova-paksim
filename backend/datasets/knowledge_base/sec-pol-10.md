# Fraud & Security Support Policy

## Scope
This approved PakSim policy covers fraud & security complaints handled by Fraud & Security.

## Standard handling
Verify the customer identity and collect the relevant service or transaction details. Apply the approved rule matrix and do not make unsupported promises.

## Categories
SIM Misuse, Suspected SIM Swap, Unauthorized Activity, Account Compromise

## Service level
Default target: 8 hours.

## Safety
Escalate security-sensitive or otherwise mandatory cases according to the rule matrix.
