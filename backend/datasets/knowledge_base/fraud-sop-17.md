# Fraud Incident Response SOP

## Purpose
This PakSim demo SOP defines handling of suspected fraud, unauthorized activity and account compromise.

## Immediate handling
Verify identity, preserve relevant transaction details and restrict disclosure of sensitive account information.

## Investigation
Record the reported event, affected service, time and available evidence.

## Escalation
Suspected SIM swap, account compromise or unauthorized activity must follow Fraud & Security escalation rules.

## Prohibited actions
Do not make unsupported claims about a fraud investigation or promise a financial outcome before verification.
