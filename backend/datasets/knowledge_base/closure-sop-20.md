# Complaint Closure & Quality SOP

## Purpose
This PakSim demo SOP defines when a complaint may be resolved or closed.

## Resolution evidence
The agent must record the action taken, relevant policy or rule and the customer-facing response.

## Confirmation
Where confirmation is required, obtain it before closure or follow the applicable closure rule.

## Quality review
Cases with validation mismatches, safety concerns or manual-review triggers must not bypass the review workflow.

## Feedback
After resolution or closure, allow the customer to provide feedback and preserve the audit history.
