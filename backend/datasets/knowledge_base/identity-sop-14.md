# Customer Identity Verification SOP

## Purpose
This PakSim demo SOP defines identity checks for account-sensitive requests.

## Verification
Use approved account identifiers and verification status. Do not disclose protected account information before verification.

## SIM and account actions
Replacement, SIM swap, ownership and account-security requests require the applicable identity checks.

## Escalation
Suspected account compromise, SIM-swap fraud or repeated failed verification must follow the fraud/security escalation rules.

## Privacy
Collect only information necessary for the requested service and keep audit records for material actions.
