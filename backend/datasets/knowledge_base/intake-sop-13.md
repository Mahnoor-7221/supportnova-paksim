# Complaint Intake & Classification SOP

## Purpose
This PakSim demo SOP defines how customer complaints are captured, classified and routed before resolution.

## Required intake
Record the customer statement, service or product, relevant reference number when available, complaint date and requested resolution. Missing information must be flagged rather than invented.

## Classification
Use the approved complaint category, subcategory and rule matrix. Multi-issue complaints must preserve each issue and must not be silently discarded.

## Routing
Route the case to the department returned by the active rule. High-risk and mandatory escalation conditions must be escalated.

## Quality
AI output must pass independent validation before it is treated as authoritative.
