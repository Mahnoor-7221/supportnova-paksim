# Network Outage Escalation SOP

## Purpose
This PakSim demo SOP defines handling of network, coverage and service-outage complaints.

## Triage
Capture location, affected service, approximate start time and whether the issue affects one user or multiple users.

## Troubleshooting
Apply approved basic troubleshooting before escalation where appropriate. Do not claim a network outage without supporting evidence.

## Escalation
Persistent or area-wide service impact must be routed to Network Operations according to the active escalation rules.

## Customer communication
Provide only verified status information and an approved follow-up expectation.
