# SIM Replacement Verification SOP

## Purpose
This PakSim demo SOP defines verification and handling for lost, stolen and replacement SIM requests.

## Verification
Confirm customer identity and account ownership using approved verification controls.

## Replacement
Record the reason, affected number and request status. Apply the current SIM replacement policy and rule matrix.

## Security
Suspected SIM swap or unauthorized replacement must be routed to Fraud & Security.

## Closure
Confirm the replacement status before closing the complaint.
